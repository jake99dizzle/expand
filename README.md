# EXPAND

A minimal arcade game for mobile browsers.

## How to Play

Circles appear on screen and expand outward. Swipe through them to destroy them before any two circles touch each other. The moment two circles make contact — it’s over.

That’s it.

## The Challenge

Circles spawn faster and grow quicker the longer you survive. The first few seconds are calm. By the time you’re past a minute, the screen is filling up fast and every swipe counts. Your goal is to keep the board clear for as long as possible.

- **Swipe** through a circle to destroy it
- **Any direction** works — slash through however feels natural
- **Two circles touching** ends the run instantly
- Your **best score** is saved between sessions

## Tips

- Don’t wait. Destroy circles early while they’re small and easy to isolate.
- Watch the edges — circles spawning near existing ones can sneak up on you.
- Long diagonal swipes can clear multiple circles at once.
- Stay calm. Panic swiping causes misses.
